# Introduction

You are invited to take part in a research study conducted by investigators from Washington University in St. Louis. The purpose of this study is to understand how different zoom interaction designs affect how people explore time-series data visualizations. The findings may help improve the design of future visualizations.

If you choose to participate, you will interact with visualizations and answer questions about the data. You will begin with a short tutorial explaining the task, the data representation (a line chart), and how to use the zoom interactions. During the main tasks, you will be asked to identify peaks in the data by marking each peak’s start, end, and highest point. You will also be asked to complete several brief survey questions. You may skip any questions you prefer not to answer. The study will take approximately 30 minutes.


You will receive a base payment of $5.00 for participating. You may earn additional compensation based on task performance and survey completion, for a maximum total payment of $8.00.

Participation is voluntary. You may choose not to participate or to stop participating at any time. There is no penalty or loss of benefits for choosing not to participate or for ending participation early. Data collected prior to withdrawal will be retained as part of the study records.


This study involves minimal risk. We will not collect your name or other directly identifying information. Interaction data (such as mouse clicks and zoom actions) and survey responses will be recorded. De-identified data may be shared with other researchers for research purposes.


We encourage you to ask questions. If you have any questions for the research team, please contact Alvitta Ottley [alvitta@wustl.edu](alvitta@wustl.edu) or you may contact the Human Research Protection Office at 1-(800)-438-0445 or [hrpo@wustl.edu](hrpo@wustl.edu).  

Thank you very much for your consideration of this research study. By proceeding, you indicate that you are at least 18 years old and that you voluntarily agree to participate in this research.
